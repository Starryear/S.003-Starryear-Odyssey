# Episode and Translation Guide

Read this reference whenever selecting the narrative, palette, or abstract vocabulary.

## Evidence-first episode routing

Choose the episode supported by several independent signals. Prefer a broader proposition over a forced literal match.

- **ATHENA KEEPS WATCH** — solitary bird/person, high viewpoint, distant witness, vigilant stillness.
- **AFTER TROY / THE DEPARTURE** — harbor, masts, crowd, wall, road leaving a dense place.
- **LOTUS SHORE** — waterbirds, flowers, slow current, quiet gathering, suspended dreamlike time.
- **CYCLOPS CAVE** — rock, aperture, round structure, one huge mass surrounding smaller marks.
- **THE WINDS UNBOUND** — clouds, airflow, spirals, fabric, radiating or suddenly divergent directions.
- **CIRCE'S ISLAND** — animals, garden/forest, seductive warm interior, altered or unstable identity.
- **SIREN OMENS** — birds, repeated sound-like lines, distant lure, goal that cannot safely be reached.
- **BETWEEN TWO MONSTERS** — constricted passage, whirlpool, jaws, tendrils, simultaneous left/right pressure.
- **CATTLE OF THE SUN** — animal group, severe sun, gold field, visibly protected or forbidden object.
- **DESCENT TO THE UNDERWORLD** — flame, darkness, reflection, bare trees, underground enclosure, smoke, red-black tension.
- **CALYPSO'S ISLAND** — one figure in a beautiful but inescapable enclosure; prolonged suspension.
- **HALL OF SUITORS** — dense crowd, tables, repeated seats, occupied hall or public space.
- **PENELOPE'S VIGIL** — window, textile, warm room, clock, empty interior, long waiting.
- **THE HIDDEN KING** — doorway, back view, occluded figure, unnoticed observer returning.
- **THE BOW TRIAL** — arcs, tension lines, aligned targets, a sudden directional reversal.
- **THE FATHER'S GARDEN** — garden, old tree, two figures, recognition after delay, quiet closure.
- **THE LONG RETURN** — coast, road, stair, lone traveler, vessel/wayfinding mark, body facing distance.

Broader propositions: **A DANGEROUS PASSAGE**, **THE WAITING HOUSE**, **BEFORE THE HOMECOMING**, **THE SEA REMEMBERS**.

## Episode-specific motif vocabulary

Use only motifs from the selected episode, and abstract them into the photograph's existing geometry.

- **Athena** — one watchful ring, a high pale node, shield-like circular pressure; avoid a literal owl unless the photo contains a bird.
- **Departure / long return** — rope tension, oar cadence, sail curvature, hull ribs, wake line, distant island notch.
- **Lotus eaters** — slow seed/oval drift, soft repetition, interrupted route, forms losing directional urgency.
- **Cyclops** — one incomplete eye ring, cave aperture, clustered smaller marks under one dominant mass.
- **Aeolus** — compressed pouch-like field, radial release lines, conflicting wind vectors.
- **Circe** — identity shift through paired forms, animal-to-human ambiguity, woven enclosure, warm threshold.
- **Sirens** — repeated waveform or feather cadence, bound central route, lure displaced beyond reach.
- **Scylla and Charybdis** — sixfold pressure on one side, consuming ring on the other, a narrow survivable channel.
- **Cattle of the Sun** — repeated horn arcs or gold-backed modules; one broken prohibition boundary.
- **Underworld** — smoke fissure, dark water threshold, bone-white witness marks, upward ember traces.
- **Calypso** — beautiful enclosure, looping path, one outward-facing isolated node.
- **Suitors** — occupied grid, repeated vessels/seats, one suppressed central axis.
- **Penelope** — woven-and-unwoven grid, returning thread, repeated day/night band.
- **Hidden king** — masked returning mark, doorway within doorway, nearly invisible bow arc.
- **Bow trial** — one taut arc crossing a sequence of aligned apertures.
- **Father's garden** — two marks gradually recognizing one another, graft line, old tree-ring structure.

## Iconic signature library

Use one signature only when its episode wins the evidence match. It should be recognizable by relationship rather than detailed depiction.

- **THE TROJAN HORSE** — one colossal dark timber volume entering or occupying a threshold; repeated wooden ribs, wheel-like low circles, a hollow belly slit, and small hidden ember marks. Never draw a complete decorative horse statue.
- **CYCLOPS CAVE** — one enormous incomplete eye ring or blazing aperture inside a cave-like mass; smaller marks cluster beneath it; bone wedges or sheep-like ovals may provide scale. Avoid a humanoid giant portrait.
- **CHARYBDIS** — one off-center consuming spiral or broken concentric void that pulls surrounding bands, nodes, and reflections inward. The vortex must visibly distort the entire field rather than appear as a small whirlpool icon.
- **SCYLLA** — six unequal neck/tendril/strike vectors emerge from one side or overhead while the route compresses against an opposing wall. Suggest six-headed danger through count and attack rhythm, not creature anatomy.
- **BETWEEN SCYLLA AND CHARYBDIS** — pair the sixfold pressure with one opposing consuming void, leaving a narrow navigable channel. This is the only route where both signatures belong together.
- **SIRENS** — repeated feather/wave/sound forms pull toward an unreachable luminous bank; a taut binding line holds the central route. Do not depict glamorous women or literal singing birds unless the photograph supports figures or birds.
- **PENELOPE'S LOOM** — a woven grid visibly unravels into loose night threads; one returning line repairs or crosses it.
- **THE BOW TRIAL** — one monumental taut bow arc aligns with a sequence of ring-like apertures and a sudden straight release vector.
- **THE UNDERWORLD** — a black water threshold, smoke column, bone-white witness marks, and ember fissures; figures remain traces, never zombie illustration.
- **THE SUN'S CATTLE** — a forbidden gold field of repeated horn arcs or body-like modules behind one broken boundary.

## Abstract translation vocabulary

- Water, road, or ground → full-frame band, tidal layer, arc, route, or interrupted passage.
- Person or animal → small upright mark, oval, seed, isolated field, or directional node.
- Architecture or doorway → dark rectangle, repeated aperture, vertical tower, layered plane.
- Mast, trunk, railing, or crowd → irregular verticals, rods, point fields, shifting density.
- Flame → broken rising band, dark-red fissure, bronze node, smoke-black route.
- Whirlpool, mouth, or strait → incomplete ring, curved clamp, consuming void.
- Eye or watcher → one high-contrast point or ring surrounded by subordinate elements.
- Reflection → shortened, faded, displaced, or broken vertical echo.
- Group → repeated modules with organic spacing and density changes.
- Light/shadow → diagonal cut, long light strip, fractured grid, or leaning plane.

## Style routing

### Cold-sea epic

Use for water, birds, harbor, storm, passage, sirens, forgetting, or sea danger. Work mainly with deep Aegean blue, blue-black, cold gray, bone white, seaweed green, and small ancient-gold/rust accents. Favor tidal bands, tracks, fine lines, mist gaps, bone fragments, wayfinding nodes, and oppressive voids.

### Odyssey candlelight

Use for thresholds, halls, fire, interiors, waiting, sacrifice, prophecy, the underworld, or danger emerging from darkness. Work with beeswax gold, brazier orange, burnt umber, dark red, oxidized bronze, smoke black, deep-sea blue, and bone white. Warmth must emerge locally from apertures, fissures, arches, scales, or dark water; never apply a global orange filter or add literal candles without source evidence.

For other episode/style routes, retain a small Odyssey ember whenever compatible: a homecoming beacon for return scenes, a dangerous lure for sirens/Circe, a forbidden glow for the cattle, a threshold glow for the hidden king, or an ember/fissure for Hades. It should illuminate material and cast echoes, not float as an unrelated orange dot.

### Monster metaphor

Use for snakes, caves, statues, fences, vortices, animal groups, giant architecture, constriction, or oppressive repetition. Suggest an unseen creature using incomplete eye rings, bone wedges, uneven holes, throat/tendril paths, compressed channels, irregular heads/nodes, swallowing fields, scale systems, bilateral pressure, or a submerged contour.

Blend styles only when different visible evidence supports each component.

## Label rules

Use exactly one correct two-to-four-word English label, plus one number and one date. Place them near a lower-panel edge with quiet editorial hierarchy. Do not add a movie name, credits, location, camera data, watermark, or extra copy.
